# Anchor High-Impact Content Before Randomizing the Remainder

## Pattern Rule
**IF** procedural generation is being used to populate content whose placement or identity does not all carry equal structural importance
**THEN** deliberately fix the elements that must support the scenario's goals, information paths, stakes, or major rewards before randomizing lower-criticality content around them
**ELSE** allow full procedural generation when no individual result must carry authored structural responsibility.

## Do
- Name which elements are load-bearing before rolling: objective locations, required clues, major opposition, signature rewards, exits, faction anchors, or other content the scenario cannot afford to misplace.
- Use random generation for texture, secondary occupants, incidental resources, local complications, and other material whose variation creates replay value without breaking the scenario.
- Let fixed and generated content interact so procedural results create new routes, pressures, or interpretations around the anchored structure.
- Recheck the final composition after generation; procedural additions can still block access, trivialize a threat, or overload one area even when the critical elements were placed deliberately.

## Don't
- Delegate a required objective, sole clue, or other progress-critical element to a table that can omit or isolate it.
- Hand-place every minor detail when variability would reduce authoring burden and increase discovery without threatening the structure.
- Treat procedural generation as neutral filler; generated content still changes pacing, resources, risk, and information.
- Repair a structurally broken result by secretly changing unrelated state after play begins when the problem could have been prevented during construction.

## Checklist
- Every scenario-critical element has been identified before procedural population begins.
- A random result can vary or disappear without removing the scenario's required operating structure.
- Generated content still has enough consequence to matter rather than functioning as decoration.
- The combined fixed and random result has been checked for blocked routes, accidental trivialization, and excessive concentration.

## Notes
Pure authored placement and pure random generation are not the only choices. Hybrid construction can reserve designer judgment for the small number of elements that carry structural responsibility while using procedural generation to supply variety and local surprise. The key boundary is impact: randomness is safest where variation changes the situation without being allowed to erase the reason the scenario works.
