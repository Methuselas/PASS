# Make Character Background Mechanically Causal

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a character's prior occupation, social position, upbringing, or other background is supposed to matter after play begins
**THEN** let that background causally determine present capabilities, possessions, permissions, obligations, advancement routes, or economic opportunities
**ELSE** keep the background primarily descriptive when later procedures do not need to distinguish it.

## Do
- Let prior life explain concrete present state such as learned skills, tools, clothing, contacts, licences, debts, professional knowledge, or access to institutions.
- Make at least some future options follow from that state: a profession can open likely advancement routes, qualify the character for work, or create plausible teachers and employers.
- Preserve acquired capabilities when the character changes occupation unless the fiction gives a clear reason they would be lost.
- Use the background as a causal answer to "why can this character do or obtain this?" rather than requiring a second disconnected origin story for the same capability.
- Keep the mechanical footprint proportional to how often the background distinction will matter in recurring play.

## Don't
- Give a background a long biography but let every character begin with the same effective skills, equipment, access, and development options.
- Treat an occupation as a decorative class label whose only effect is a small numeric bonus unrelated to what that occupation does in the world.
- Make a later career change erase skills or practical knowledge that the character already established through play without an explicit loss mechanism.
- Add background-specific rules that never affect a decision merely to make the sheet look more detailed.

## Checklist
- At least one present capability, possession, permission, obligation, or access condition follows directly from the chosen or generated background.
- At least one later decision can differ because of that background without inventing a new justification at the moment of use.
- The mechanical consequences match what the background fiction says the character has actually done or learned.
- Changing occupation does not silently delete already earned state.
- Removing the background mechanics would materially weaken the connection between character history and present play.

## Notes
A background becomes part of the game when it leaves executable state behind. An occupation can explain what a character knows, what equipment they plausibly own, which work they can find, which institutions recognize them, and which next careers are credible. This is distinct from leaving unresolved personal hooks for later scenarios: a background can be fully settled biography and still matter because it caused the character's current mechanical position.
