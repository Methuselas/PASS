# Preserve Deliberate Setting Negative Space for Downstream Ownership

## Pattern Rule
**IF** an established setting leaves a region, institution, history, system, boundary, or other area undefined so referees or later creators can extend it
**THEN** preserve that negative space unless new canon creates more playable relationships, pressures, or useful context than the downstream ownership it removes
**ELSE** define the area normally when no meaningful authorship value depends on leaving it open.

## Do
- Treat an undefined area as a possible design affordance rather than automatically as missing work.
- Identify who is expected to own the blank in use: the referee, campaign designer, downstream author, or another creator.
- Add canon when the new material creates useful relationships, pressures, adventure context, or causal density worth the ownership cost.
- Prefer additions that make existing elements more connected without needlessly closing unrelated blanks.
- Keep enough stable anchors around open space that downstream additions can still belong coherently to the setting.

## Don't
- Fill every undefined region, institution, history, or cultural blank merely because an expansion has room to do so.
- Treat encyclopedic completeness as automatically more useful than adaptable open space.
- Close a blank that downstream creators are expected to own without identifying what the new canon buys in play.
- Leave open space so unconstrained that later additions have no stable setting anchors to connect to.

## Checklist
- The blank has a recognizable downstream ownership or extension function rather than existing only from neglect.
- New canon added inside previously open space creates usable relationships, pressures, or playable context.
- The gain from the addition is weighed against the authorship space it removes.
- Adjacent open areas remain available when the new material does not need to define them.
- Stable setting anchors remain sufficient for downstream material to integrate coherently.

## Notes
Undefined setting space can be intentional capacity. An expansion is not automatically stronger because it fills every blank; some blanks exist so referees or later authors can place their own institutions, histories, borders, or local answers. Add canon when it makes the world denser and more causally useful, not merely more complete.
