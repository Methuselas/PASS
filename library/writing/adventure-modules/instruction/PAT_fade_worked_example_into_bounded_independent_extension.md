# Fade a Worked Example into Bounded Independent Extension

## Pattern Rule
**IF** an introductory adventure module should teach the operator to customize or author adjacent material rather than remain dependent on the supplied scenario
**THEN** demonstrate one bounded construction task with visible reasoning, hand the operator a closely related completion task with explicit constraints, and only then widen the scope toward independent extension
**ELSE** provide finished material without a teaching progression when author development is not part of the module's purpose

## Do
- Choose a contained construction problem whose reasoning will recur in later authoring, such as expanding a keyed location, detailing a building, extending a map, or adapting a prepared situation.
- Show the reasoning that converts known facts into design decisions instead of presenting only a polished finished example.
- Preserve concrete constraints in the demonstration: dimensions, connections, functions, inhabitants, access, recurring needs, or other facts the extension must continue to satisfy.
- After modeling part of the task, assign a nearby analogous portion for the operator to complete and state the few facts that must remain true.
- Let the novice make genuine choices inside those constraints; do not provide the answer immediately after inviting independent work.
- Widen the next invitation only after the smaller task is understandable, moving from completion to customization and then to new adjacent material.
- Point back to the worked example as a model the operator can reuse rather than inventing a different procedure for each extension.

## Don't
- Show only a finished map, scene, or key and call it a worked example when the reasoning that produced it is invisible.
- Jump directly from "run this prepared module" to "design your own campaign" without an intermediate task small enough to attempt safely.
- Remove every constraint from the independent step; unrestricted invention does not reveal whether the novice understood the demonstrated method.
- Make the extension exercise a tracing task whose choices are already determined by the example.
- Teach decorative embellishment as if it were the same skill as preserving functional relationships.
- Require completion of the teaching exercise before the supplied adventure can be run unless the exercise itself is essential preparation.

## Checklist
- The worked example exposes at least two reasons behind its design choices, not just the resulting artifact.
- The independent completion task is similar enough that the demonstrated reasoning transfers.
- The task states which constraints must remain true while leaving at least one meaningful choice to the operator.
- A successful completion can be checked against functions and relationships rather than against one hidden canonical answer.
- The progression moves from supplied example to bounded completion before asking for a larger original extension.
- The ready-made scenario remains usable even if the operator postpones optional design practice.

## Notes
A good introductory module can become a small apprenticeship in authorship. The useful move is a fading handoff: first the text reasons through a concrete example, then it stops partway through a similar problem and names the constraints the novice must preserve, then it invites broader additions once those relationships are visible.

This is stronger than simply telling the operator to "make it your own." Personalization without a model rewards prior experience. A faded worked example exposes why a prepared feature is shaped as it is, gives the novice a tractable place to exercise judgment, and turns the module from a consumable scenario into a bridge toward independent construction.
