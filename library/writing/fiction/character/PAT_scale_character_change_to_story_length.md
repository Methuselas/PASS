# Scale Character Change to Story Length

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a fiction ending forces a dramatic transformation that the available length has not earned
**THEN** scale the change to a credible realization, revision, understanding, or decision and let the reader infer consequences beyond the final scene
**ELSE** retain a larger transformation only when accumulated choices and pressure make it both surprising and consistent

## Do
- Identify the smallest shift that changes how the character sees a person, situation, or next choice.
- Express change through a revised action, withheld habit, new observation, or altered response to a repeated condition.
- In flash fiction, end on evidence of the shift rather than narrating the life that follows from it.
- Trace the ending back through earlier pressures to confirm that the new response grows from the established person.

## Don't
- Add catastrophe solely to make the ending feel important.
- Make a previously stable trait vanish because the story needs closure.
- Announce the lesson in a grand explanatory speech after behavior has already implied it.
- Confuse a change in external circumstance with a change in the character's understanding or conduct.

## Checklist
- The ending alters perception, intention, or behavior rather than merely supplying new information.
- The size of the shift fits the story's accumulated duration and pressure.
- Earlier details make the change convincing without making it inevitable.
- The final scene supplies evidence from which later effects can be inferred.
- Removing the explanatory sentence, if any, does not erase the change.

## Notes
Short fiction does not require a total conversion. A slight revision can be structurally large when it changes the next choice. The relevant measure is not spectacle but whether the ending exposes a new relation between the established person and the story's pressure.

`writing_fiction_variant_separate_recognition_from_reform` prevents an accurate self-diagnosis from doing the work of an arc. Name which state the story has actually reached: recognition, intention, confession, changed action, repeated changed action, or durable reform. Re-test the old pressure or temptation when the length permits, and ask whether the new conduct depends on protection, distance, a changed social environment, or another support that may not persist. A story may end on recognition or the beginning of change; it should simply avoid naming that threshold as completed reform.

`writing_fiction_variant_separate_compliance_belief_and_allegiance` adds domain resolution to deep transformation. Track what the character performs, says, propositionally believes, emotionally feels, desires, remains attached to, identifies with, and automatically monitors. These layers can change in different orders; use only the distinctions the story has room to demonstrate, and do not claim total conversion from movement in one layer.
